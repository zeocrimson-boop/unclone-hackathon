Privacy, sources & limitations

Three independent signals

EchoGuard uses transparent English-language rules to assess pasted text. These are editorial triage rules, not a trained AI model or calibrated probabilities. Negation, quoted speech and unfamiliar tactics can be misread. No warning signs does not prove safety.

When configured, DetectifAI assesses indications of synthetic speech. Its verdict cannot identify a speaker or establish fraud. Phone-quality audio can be less reliable. The provider is tuned for English; speaker verification is a separate service and is not included here.

Community reports are unverified allegations. A number may be spoofed or reassigned. Counts represent submissions, not distinct people or proof of wrongdoing. No reports found does not mean a number is safe.

Audio and text privacy

Audio is limited to 10 MB and kept in request-scoped memory; EchoGuard does not write recordings to disk or permanently store them. Buffers are cleared after processing, but this is not a guarantee of forensic memory erasure. When enabled, audio is sent to DetectifAI. Review the provider’s privacy policy before uploading sensitive recordings. Do not assume that an external provider has no retention.

Pasted text is processed locally on the server and is not saved by EchoGuard. Remove names, account details, authentication codes and other private information before submitting. No automatic audio transcription is provided. Hosting providers may retain operational metadata according to their policies.

Community data and review

Reports store a normalized phone number, category, optional private description, timestamp and visibility status in PostgreSQL. Descriptions are never published by the public API. Do not submit names, financial information, OTPs or recordings. No reverse-number lookups or identity searches are performed.

Abuse prevention stores a daily rotating HMAC hash of the connection address rather than the address itself. A shared connection may share limits. Abuse windows and report hashes expire from active storage after two days when submissions next run. Backups may retain older data. Reports remain until the operator removes them under its retention policy; operators must review and set a suitable policy before public launch.

Use “Request review” after searching a number to flag a misleading or privacy-sensitive result. Requests enter a private review queue; removal is not automatic. The operator must regularly review that queue. For a private description requiring correction, use the deployment operator’s support channel; this prototype does not collect reporter identities or offer authenticated self-service deletion.

Missing services and uncertainty

Text analysis works without credentials. Voice analysis requires a DetectifAI key with the correct scope and credits. Community reporting requires a migrated database and abuse-prevention secret. Service failures are displayed as errors, not successful safe assessments.
