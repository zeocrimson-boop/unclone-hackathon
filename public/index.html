    BEFORE YOU INVESTIGATE

    Clarity about our limits.

    Does synthetic speech mean a caller is a scammer?

    No. Voice authenticity, message content and community reports answer different questions. EchoGuard never combines them into a scam probability.

    Can I analyze text without an API key?

    Yes. Paste a message, offer, email, or manually obtained transcript. Transparent English-language rules run on the server without an external text service. They can miss scams or misread context.

    Will EchoGuard transcribe or store my recording?

    No automatic transcription is provided. Audio is processed in request-scoped memory and is not permanently stored by EchoGuard. When configured, DetectifAI receives the audio; its own retention policy applies.

    What does a community report prove?

    Only that someone submitted a report. Numbers can be spoofed or reassigned. Reports do not identify a person or establish wrongdoing. Flag misleading results for operator review.

    What should I do if I have already paid or shared an OTP?

    Contact your bank through its official channel immediately, secure affected accounts, preserve evidence, and contact your local cybercrime reporting service. In India, call 1930 for financial cybercrime assistance.
